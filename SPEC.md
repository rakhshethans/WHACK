# WorldHopper product specification

WorldHopper turns the skills people are learning into islands that develop as they practise. It is a WarwickHack 2026 entry for Marshall Wace’s New Worlds challenge.

## First playable slice

- Start with an empty ocean and create a guitar island from an experience level, objective and practice window.
- See four ordered goals. Completing an unlocked goal awards XP once and unlocks the next. The first completion upgrades the settlement to a village.
- See five distinct island stages and resume progress after a reload on the same browser.
- Explore two labelled sample worlds and adopt their roadmaps with zero XP and attribution to the sample creator.

Use an illustrated nautical atlas with teal water, warm terrain and an isometric island. Keep the map central and place the current roadmap beside it or below it on small screens.

[Habitica](https://habitica.com/) already demonstrates that task completion and social game mechanics can coexist. WorldHopper’s distinguishing flow is to visualise progress separately for each skill and let people discover and reuse a specific roadmap.

## Full MVP acceptance

Use validated local model output when it improves goal quality. Keep curated roadmaps available on unsupported hardware and when output fails validation. Compare goal quality across 20–30 fictional users. Add explainable skill and profile discovery. Introduce real profiles and follows only once opt-in visibility and access controls can be verified. Goal completion is self-reported. The demo should run on a free tier and recover gracefully without WebGPU.
