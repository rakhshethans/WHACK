# WorldHopper demo

“WorldHopper turns personal growth into a world you can explore.”

1. Start with the empty ocean. Choose **New island** and name it Guitar Island.
2. Enter “Play a song for friends”, choose **New to it** and select a 20-minute session.
3. Show how the roadmap moves from clean chords to a full take. Complete the first goal and show the village appear at 100 XP.
4. Reload and confirm the village and completed goal persist.
5. Open **Explore**, select a labelled sample world and adopt its roadmap.
6. Show that the adopted island keeps its creator credit and begins at Level 1, with its own progression.

Use a fresh browser profile for the empty-ocean opening. This build stores progress in the browser and uses curated roadmaps, so the demonstration requires no account or AI download.
